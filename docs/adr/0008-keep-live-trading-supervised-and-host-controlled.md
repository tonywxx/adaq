# Keep live trading supervised and host-controlled

ADAQ V1 contains no real account or order execution; the first live milestone requires Supervised Local Execution through direct desktop-to-Venue connections and provides no cloud or unattended executor. All manual and Strategy actions pass through fail-closed host Risk and OMS controls under one Live Control Lease per Venue account and Instrument; the Venue is authoritative for balances, orders, and fills, SQLite is the durable local journal, startup and reconnect require reconciliation before any resend, and withdrawal-enabled credentials are rejected and stored only in the operating-system keychain. Faults cancel pending orders, pause the Strategy Instance, and retain the position by default; users may choose Stop and Keep Position or Stop and Flatten, while Freeze All and separately confirmed Flatten All provide system-wide emergency control and reconciliation.
