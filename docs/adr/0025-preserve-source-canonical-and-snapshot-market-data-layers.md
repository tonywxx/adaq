# Preserve source, canonical, and snapshot market-data layers

AdaQ V1 retains provider-delivered Venue records and acquisition provenance in an immutable Source Market Dataset bound to its Market Data Connector and Provider Capability Snapshot, derives an immutable Canonical Market Dataset under exact recorded quality rules, and records a Data Quality Report binding that input to its output. Canonicalization normalizes identities, times, and values, collapses identical duplicates, quarantines conflicts or invalid records, and preserves Bar Gaps instead of silently filling or overwriting evidence. Market Data Snapshots select content-addressed Canonical Market Data for reproducible research and execution, while research preprocessing produces separately identified derived data and never mutates Source or Canonical evidence; the additional storage is accepted so provider limitations and quality rules can be audited and rerun.
