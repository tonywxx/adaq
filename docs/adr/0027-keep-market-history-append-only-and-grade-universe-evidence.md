# Keep market history append-only and grade Universe evidence

AdaQ V1 backfills the maximum provider-retrievable one-minute history for each OKX Spot Instrument through resumable background work, appends newly confirmed Closed Bars, freezes Instrument Master Snapshots at initial collection, daily, and whenever a status change is detected, and reconciles realtime Bars against REST evidence to expose or repair acquisition gaps through new evidence rather than overwriting history. Source or Canonical revisions receive new identities, while existing Market Data Snapshots and any data referenced by a Dataset, Run, Report, or Bot remain immutable and deletion-locked. Point-in-Time Instrument Universes record an Evidence State of Observed when contemporaneous Instrument Master evidence exists, Reconstructed when membership is inferred from incomplete historical evidence, or Unknown when it cannot be established; only Observed periods support a claim of no known survivorship bias, while the other states remain usable with explicit warnings.
