# Align market semantics to Venue calendars and store UTC instants

AdaQ defines trading rules, Trading Dates, Trading Sessions, Session Phases, and Bar boundaries in each Venue's IANA time zone while storing every event and Bar boundary as a unique UTC instant. China A-share rules therefore remain aligned to `Asia/Shanghai`, United States equity rules remain aligned to `America/New_York` with time-zone database handling rather than a fixed offset, and continuously traded crypto remains aligned to its recorded UTC grid. Session-based observations bind an immutable Trading Calendar Snapshot, Trading Date, and Session Phase; holidays, scheduled breaks, and early closes are calendar evidence rather than Bar Gaps. Intraday Bars align to Venue-local sessions, daily and longer Bars align to Venue-local Trading Dates, and the UI defaults to Venue-local display without changing canonical identity. This dual representation adds explicit calendar evidence but prevents daylight-saving changes, device time zones, UTC date boundaries, and scheduled market closures from corrupting joins, gaps, Features, Backtests, or Paper Trading.
