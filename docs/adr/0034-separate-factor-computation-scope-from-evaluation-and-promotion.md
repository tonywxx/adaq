# Separate Factor computation scope from evaluation and promotion

AdaQ V1 adds one host-owned Factor Lab that treats Factor Scope, Evaluation Lens, historical evidence, and User promotion as separate concepts. A Factor Evaluation Protocol freezes the exact candidate or Factor Dataset, Targets and horizons, Market Data Snapshot, Feature Plan, Point-in-Time Instrument Universe, chronological or walk-forward windows, purge and embargo, neutralization, cost and portfolio-sort assumptions, and Factor Research Family trial identity before computation. Temporal lenses measure within-Instrument predictive behavior, Cross-Sectional lenses measure same-time IC, Rank IC, breadth, quantile monotonicity, turnover, and neutralized behavior, and Economic lenses run standardized cost-aware portfolio sorts; a Time-Series Factor may use any compatible lens rather than having its computation Scope dictate its test. Reports retain coverage, missingness, stability, decay, subperiod and regime results, diagnostics, multiple-testing adjustments, and an Out-of-sample, Overlapping, or Unknown Evaluation Evidence State. The engine never reduces this evidence to an automatic `valid` flag: an immutable User-owned Factor Promotion Decision separately records Approved or Rejected, cites exact Reports and policy, and makes no guarantee of future profitability. Failed and superseded trials remain in their Research Family so repeated searches cannot erase selection-bias evidence.
