# Use Qlib-first research with ADAQ-authoritative deployment

ADAQ V1 uses Microsoft Qlib as the default Research Engine while retaining ADAQ Native as a selectable alternative on each immutable research protocol. Both engines consume ADAQ-owned Canonical Market, Feature, and Factor evidence; Qlib's data downloader does not establish a second market-data truth, and every output preserves its exact Research Engine Provenance. Qlib and ADAQ Native feature, factor, model, and research-backtest results remain separately labelled because their calendars, portfolio construction, execution, and metric semantics may differ; they may be compared side by side but are never silently merged into one report. Existing M1-M8 ADAQ capabilities remain the production-qualification path rather than becoming discarded legacy code: Qlib-generated candidates must be replayable from frozen ADAQ Features, exported through a supported Model or Component boundary, pass Component Equivalence, and pass an ADAQ Backtest and Validation before Paper Trading. Qlib, pandas, Python, framework-specific types, arbitrary notebook code, and Qlib-native strategy actions do not enter the Component ABI or directly control a Bot. This makes Qlib the more complete default research experience without transferring ADAQ's authority over data identity, evidence, packages, deployment, risk, or trading.
