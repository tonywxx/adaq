# Separate Strategy intent, host Risk, and Execution

ADAQ V1 separates the trading decision path into Strategy Target, host-owned Risk Decision, Approved Target, and Execution Plan. A Strategy owns signal interpretation, selection, desired allocation, rebalance intent, and strategy-level soft exits; it never owns credentials, orders, or non-bypassable platform limits. An immutable Risk Policy evaluates the original target and Portfolio State as Approve, Constrain, or Reject, may only preserve or reduce requested risk, records every reason, and never silently reallocates constrained capital into unrequested exposure. An Execution Profile alone converts the Approved Target into venue-valid order intentions with thresholds, rounding, order policy, expected costs, and lifecycle rules. Hard loss limits, market eligibility, settlement restrictions, and emergency controls remain host-owned even when a Strategy implements its own stop logic. Backtest and Paper Trading freeze the exact Strategy, Risk Policy, and Execution Profile and retain each intermediate decision so users can distinguish research intent, enforced safety, planned orders, and actual Fills.
