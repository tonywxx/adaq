# Keep Paper Portfolios account- and currency-scoped

ADAQ V1 binds every Paper Portfolio and Portfolio Strategy to exactly one Paper Trading Account and one Valuation Currency. The initial funding targets are CNY 1,000,000 for the A-share simulator, USD 1,000,000 for Alpaca Paper, and USDT 1,000,000 for Crypto Paper, all represented as exact decimals. An external provider's observed Paper Account Snapshot remains authoritative when it differs from the target; ADAQ displays the discrepancy and never fabricates or silently overwrites broker state. A Portfolio may coordinate many Instruments available within its Account but cannot share capital or place one allocation across A-share, U.S. equity, and crypto Accounts. The Dashboard presents native-currency account and Bot results side by side and does not add CNY, USD, and USDT into a false global total. A later Global Portfolio requires immutable FX Snapshots, a declared reporting currency, cross-account funding and settlement rules, and coordinated execution evidence rather than a display-only conversion.
