# Use provider Paper accounts and a local A-share simulator

ADAQ V1 implements an internal `adaq-paper-trading-core` evidence and lifecycle contract with three provider-specific Paper Execution Adapters: `adaq-okx-paper` targets OKX Demo Trading, `adaq-alpaca-paper` targets Alpaca Paper, and `adaq-a-share-paper` owns the local A-share simulator and ledger. This internal contract does not create the deferred public Unified Trading API. External provider Account Snapshots, order IDs, events, rejections, and Fills remain authoritative and retain their native payload provenance alongside normalized ADAQ evidence; the local simulator's immutable ledger is authoritative for A-shares. Every connection freezes a Paper Execution Capability Snapshot rather than assuming providers share order types, fill rules, sessions, shorting, buying power, or reset behavior. Credentials remain outside research records and Components. Startup, reconnect, sequence gaps, and detected inconsistencies require Paper Account Reconciliation from provider snapshots and ordered events, and unresolved divergence blocks new risk. This mixed topology exercises real provider authentication, networking, rejection, and lifecycle behavior without inventing a nonexistent free A-share Paper API or weakening ADAQ's common evidence boundary.
