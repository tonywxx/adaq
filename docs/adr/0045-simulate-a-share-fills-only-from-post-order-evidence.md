# Simulate A-share Fills only from post-order evidence

The V1 `adaq-a-share-paper` Adapter uses a dedicated event-driven A-share Paper Fill Engine rather than the historical Backtest Fill model. It may advance an accepted Paper Order only from market observations received after that acceptance and under exact Trading Calendar, Market Rule Snapshot, Execution Profile, and liquidity-participation evidence. A marketable buy is bounded by an observed executable Ask plus declared adverse slippage and a sell by the Bid minus adverse slippage; a non-marketable limit waits for later crossing evidence. Visible quantity caps fills, while missing size uses a frozen conservative participation policy and never implies unlimited liquidity. Minute or larger Bars are degraded Bar Constrained evidence and cannot retroactively provide the best price or prove queue priority; an auction Fill requires an observed clearing result. Every partial Fill records Trade Observed, Quote Constrained, or Bar Constrained provenance, while insufficient evidence remains Unavailable and produces no Fill. Order and Fill events are append-only, and T+1, sessions, lots, price limits, halts, and fees come from effective-time Market Rule Snapshots rather than constants embedded in Strategy or simulator code.
