# Support only A-share ordinary securities accounts in V1

The V1 `adaq-a-share-paper` Adapter simulates one A-share Ordinary Securities Account funded with CNY 1,000,000 and does not implement an A-share Credit Account. The Ordinary Securities Account may buy only from available own cash and sell only eligible owned quantity under the effective Trading Calendar and Market Rule Snapshots; cash reservations, T+1 availability, lots, price limits, fees, taxes, corporate actions, and every ledger transition remain explicit evidence. Its Paper Execution Capability Snapshot declares financing, securities lending, short sales, credit limits, collateral, liabilities, interest, maintenance ratios, repayment, margin calls, and forced liquidation unavailable, and Host Risk rejects any order requiring those capabilities before submission. Negative cash or positions never approximate a Credit Account. Credit trading requires a future account profile, risk model, liability ledger, settlement contract, simulator evidence model, and dedicated qualification work rather than feature flags on the ordinary-account ledger.
