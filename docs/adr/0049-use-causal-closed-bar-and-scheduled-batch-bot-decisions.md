# Use causal Closed-Bar and scheduled-batch Bot decisions

ADAQ V1 Trading Bots evaluate Strategies only through immutable Bot Decision Schedules that trigger on confirmed Closed Bars or declared Venue-local scheduled cross-sectional batch boundaries. Each invocation freezes one Decision Time and scope-correct Decision Batch; every Feature, Factor, Forecast Signal, and fitted transformation consumed by it must preserve identity and satisfy `Available At <= Decision Time`. A Time-Series decision belongs to one Instrument and Closed Bar, while a Cross-Sectional decision binds one deterministic Point-in-Time Instrument Universe and explicit missingness without silently omitting late members. Warmup, Bar Gaps, incomplete required inputs, late Models, and missed Decision Deadlines produce no new Strategy Target; the current exposure remains until a later valid decision or host Risk action. A target derived from Closed Bar `t` cannot be executed using that Bar's close, low, high, or any earlier event and may reach execution only from the next eligible post-decision market event. Venue calendars and time zones govern schedules for A-shares and U.S. equities, while Crypto uses its recorded UTC grid. Realtime Ticker, Trade, Quote, and Level 2 evidence remains available for staleness checks, hard Risk, price protection, liquidity, Paper Fill simulation, and monitoring, but cannot trigger or enter V1 Strategy decisions. Tick-driven Strategies, historical order-book replay, market making, sub-minute HFT, queue models, and their distinct ABI and qualification requirements are deferred.
