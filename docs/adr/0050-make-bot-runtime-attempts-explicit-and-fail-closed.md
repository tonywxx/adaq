# Make Bot Runtime Attempts explicit and fail closed

Every explicit Trading Bot Start or Retry creates a new immutable-identity Bot Runtime Attempt bound to one exact Bot Deployment Bundle and Worker binary. Its append-only lifecycle is Stopped, Starting, Reconciling, WarmingUp, Running, Pausing, Paused, Stopping, or Faulted; Stopped and Faulted are terminal for that Attempt, and only Running may authorize a new risk-increasing Strategy Target. Starting validates the Bundle and runtime, Reconciling establishes authoritative Paper Account and order state, and WarmingUp reconstructs analytical state before Running. Pause immediately blocks new targets and enters Paused only after eligible pending orders are cancelled and account state is reconciled; otherwise the Attempt becomes or remains Faulted with Reconciliation Required. Resume always passes through Reconciling and WarmingUp and never replays a stale pre-pause target. Stop and Keep Position is the default and leaves any holding as an explicit Unmanaged Position, while separately confirmed Stop and Flatten must complete host-controlled liquidation and reconciliation before Stopped. Worker, Model, data, network, deadline, Adapter, or reconciliation faults fail closed, retain exact evidence, and never automatically return to Running; technical reconnect or reconstruction may proceed, but operator Resume or Retry is required in supervised V1. Lifecycle State remains distinct from Health severity so a warning cannot ambiguously grant order authority.
