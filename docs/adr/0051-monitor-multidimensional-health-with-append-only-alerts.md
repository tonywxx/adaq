# Monitor multidimensional Health with append-only Alerts

ADAQ V1 places one host-owned Monitoring Engine beside the Bot Supervisor and evaluates Market Data, Worker, Feature/Model/Strategy, Paper Account, Risk/OMS, Execution Adapter, Local System, and Research Feedback as independent Bot Health Dimensions. Each Dimension is Healthy, Degraded, Critical, or Unknown; Overall Bot Health is the worst required Dimension for triage, but Lifecycle State remains the sole runtime-authority state and every underlying Dimension remains visible. Unknown fails closed when a required dependency cannot be established. Workers and Adapters emit typed observations but cannot self-declare authority; the host writes append-only Operational Events to SQLite, derives rebuildable current projections, and retains bounded metric rollups rather than every market tick. Frozen Alert Policies create deduplicated Info, Warning, or Critical Operational Alerts with Active, Acknowledged, and Resolved lifecycle Events; acknowledgement never implies resolution, and debounce, occurrence thresholds, hysteresis, deduplication, and cooldown prevent alert storms without suppressing first Critical evidence. Provider health distinguishes device connectivity, DNS/TLS, REST, market streams, account streams, data freshness, rate limits, and clock drift rather than trusting a generic Internet ping. Linked Monitoring Safety Actions may skip one Decision, enter Pausing, terminate an Attempt as Faulted with Reconciliation Required, or invoke Freeze All for journal, disk, or clock-integrity failure. Factor decay, Model drift, and Paper-performance divergence create Research Review warnings and never automatically retrain, promote, replace, or redeploy a Component. V1 delivers alerts through the localized GUI Notification Center, persistent Critical banners, and operating-system notifications; remote email, SMS, chat, cloud telemetry, and a Prometheus deployment are deferred.
