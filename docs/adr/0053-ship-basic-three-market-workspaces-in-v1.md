# Ship basic three-market Workspaces in V1

ADAQ V1 adds one localized Markets sidebar group with `/markets`, `/markets/crypto`, `/markets/a-shares`, and `/markets/us-equities`. The existing Crypto Watchlist, Ticker, and Kline page moves from `/` to the Crypto route, while `/` remains the Operations Dashboard. All Market Workspaces reuse one asset-class-neutral, User-scoped Watchlist keyed by exact Instrument ID and share the host's canonical search, session, Ticker, Bar, provider-coverage, freshness, Data Quality, and rule-summary contracts; each route filters by its exact Venue or Asset Class without conflating display symbols. Crypto shows OKX Spot evidence, A-shares show `akshare-rs` plus actual upstream provenance and applicable board, status, price-limit, lot, T+1, and Unknown-rule states, and U.S. equities show Alpaca feed identity and coverage or delay together with session and Corporate Action evidence; auxiliary `yfinance-rs` data never masquerades as authoritative realtime. Each page paints immediately, uses owning-control loading, reuses current-session user-scoped read caches on re-entry, refreshes in the background, and exposes data age and provenance. V1 permits navigation from an Instrument to applicable research, Backtest, or Bot configuration but does not turn the market view into direct order entry. Advanced screening, full fundamentals and statements, news and alternative data, historical Level 2 replay, DOM, advanced drawing and multi-chart terminals, derivatives, and cross-market arbitrage workspaces are post-V1.
