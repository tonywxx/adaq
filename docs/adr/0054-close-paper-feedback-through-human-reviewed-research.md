# Close Paper feedback through human-reviewed research

ADAQ V1 closes the Paper Trading feedback loop through immutable Paper Feedback Snapshots, lens-specific Paper Feedback Reports, and explicit User-owned Research Review Decisions rather than automatic retraining or hot replacement. A Snapshot binds one exact Bot Deployment Bundle and time range to its market observations, Decision Batches, Feature and Forecast outputs, Strategy Targets, Risk Decisions, Orders, Fills, Paper Account and reconciliation events, operational conditions, realized outcomes, and completeness evidence. Factor reports examine scope-correct IC, Rank IC, coverage, turnover, decay, and regime behavior; Model reports examine distribution, calibration or error, predictive statistics, Feature drift, and inference latency; Strategy reports examine native-currency return, drawdown, exposure, turnover, Risk interventions, and like-for-like Backtest divergence; Execution reports examine acknowledgements, rejects, partial Fills, slippage, Fill Evidence State, cancellation, reconciliation, and provider latency. Metrics remain Not Yet Realized or Insufficient Evidence until their exact horizons and frozen sample thresholds are satisfied, and late observations never backdate an earlier window. Paper Fills and account events stay execution evidence rather than Canonical Market Data, while newly observed market records may enter a new Source Market Dataset revision without rewriting prior Snapshots. Drift or decay creates a Research Review Required Alert only. A Review Decision may take no action, Pause a Bot, or launch a new Factor Evaluation, Model Training, Strategy Backtest, or restructuring Attempt; any resulting change must pass the ordinary promotion, Component Equivalence, Backtest, Validation, runtime, and Deployment Qualification gates and creates a new Deployment Bundle. The active Bundle is never mutated, automatically switched to a challenger, or declared repaired by acknowledging an Alert.
