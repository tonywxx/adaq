# Keep Factor research evidence immutable and promotion User-owned

ADAQ M11 materializes exact Factor Candidates into immutable Factor Datasets before evaluation, freezes every Target, market context, window, Lens, cost, neutralization, regime, Research Family lineage, and Promotion Policy in content-addressed Protocols, and retains failed, cancelled, rejected, and superseded Trials. Reports never collapse evidence into an automatic validity flag: only an immutable User-owned Factor Promotion Decision may mark one exact output Rejected, Research Validated, or Component Eligible, and only Out-of-sample evidence may satisfy the latter two states. This costs more retained evidence and prevents automatic “best score” promotion, but preserves selection-bias history, makes multiple-testing adjustments auditable, and keeps research quality separate from Component qualification or profitability claims.
