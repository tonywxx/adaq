# Extract post-M12 Research Queue scheduling into a native module

Status: accepted

After M11 and M12 are complete, ADAQ may optimize the existing shared Research Queue without reopening either milestone's contracts. The optimization extracts scheduling mechanics from the current `Features` host into a Tauri-independent native module at `src-tauri/src/research_queue.rs`; it preserves completed M11/M12 evidence ownership, Python Control Plane lifecycle, public APIs, and the existing separation between Research Queue execution and Environment preparation.

The extracted module owns one worker, durable admission order, journal reconciliation, adapter registration, wakeup, and shutdown coordination. Feature, Factor, Python, and future Model or Strategy adapters retain their Attempt lifecycles, cancellation, reset, recovery, Stores, and evidence. A device-level Queue journal assigns an idempotent monotonic admission sequence after an Attempt is durably Pending; restart repairs missing tickets without silently reordering work, and orphan or terminal entries become tombstones. The Queue selects the earliest journal entry and routes it through a typed adapter registry, replacing per-kind `next_pending` merging and the runner `Work` enum. The native implementation lives in `src-tauri/src/research_queue.rs` and contains no Tauri or domain-store dependency.

Adapters return consumed, stale, or retryable scheduling outcomes. The Queue does not write domain terminal states, automatically retry business Attempts, silently skip a valid head, or expose User reset operations. Shutdown stops dispatch, requests cooperative adapter shutdown, preserves Pending work, and leaves interrupted Running recovery to the owning domain. Every post-M12 heavy Runner-spawning research Attempt uses this Queue; pure reads and validation may remain direct. An unavailable or incompatible Queue journal fails closed for heavy research without deleting completed milestone evidence or triggering an implicit rerun.
