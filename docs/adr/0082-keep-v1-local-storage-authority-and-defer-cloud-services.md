# Keep V1 operational evidence local, typed, bounded, and fail-closed

Status: accepted

ADAQ V1 keeps authoritative immutable domain evidence in its owning stores: content-addressed Parquet for Market, Feature, Factor, Model, and Signal datasets, and append-only domain journals for Paper, Orders, Fills, and lifecycle Attempts. Structured Operational Events are typed, append-only SQLite records for host observations, lifecycle transitions, operator actions, and safety actions; they reference domain identities and evidence hashes rather than duplicating domain payloads. Diagnostic Logs are bounded, attempt-scoped, unstructured records for troubleshooting. Provider-native Diagnostics preserve provider codes, request/response meanings, and sequence details after host redaction; they never become Canonical Market Data or silently replace an ADAQ decision. Numeric Operational Metrics are bounded samples or rollups under a Host retention policy, not an unbounded time-series archive. Health, Dashboard, and other read models are rebuildable projections. Alerts retain an append-only Active, Acknowledged, and Resolved lifecycle, while GUI and OS notifications are delivery projections and never the source of truth.

Every retained Event, Diagnostic, Metric sample or Alert carries a stable event identity plus the applicable User, Bot, Runtime Attempt, Domain Attempt, Provider, Account, Instrument, and observation/recording timestamps. `correlation_id` groups one causal operation, `causation_id` links a derived record to its trigger, and evidence IDs or content hashes provide exact drill-down without copying large payloads. Redaction occurs at the Host trust boundary and removes credentials, tokens, authorization headers, secret references, private paths, and other configured sensitive values before persistence, display, export, or notification. Logs and metrics are capped; truncation and rollup are themselves recorded as safe diagnostics. Unknown or Critical state for a required Health Dimension blocks new risk, and a missing journal, invalid observation, or unavailable Monitoring Engine cannot grant authority. A failed OS notification never suppresses a persisted Alert, and acknowledging an Alert never marks its condition recovered.

Retention follows evidence ownership and operational responsibility. Immutable domain evidence remains subject to its owning retention and deletion-lock rules. Operational Events, Alert lifecycle, and safety-action history remain available for every linked Attempt and unresolved responsibility, then may be compacted only by an explicit bounded local policy that preserves identity and terminal outcome. Logs are retained only within per-Attempt and total-size caps; high-frequency Metrics use bounded recent samples and deterministic rollups. V1 therefore needs only the existing User-scoped SQLite metadata store, local Parquet, and OS notification integration; it does not require Prometheus, Grafana, Alertmanager, a cloud telemetry service, or a centralized hot/historical data service. Any future export or remote observability layer is a non-authoritative projection behind an explicit capability and redaction policy.

## Consequences

- SQLite and Parquet together define the local evidence boundary; summaries, indexes, DuckDB tables, Health projections, and notification state may be discarded and rebuilt.
- The existing [Monitoring and Alerting Guide](../monitoring-and-alerting.md) is the user-facing lifecycle and fail-closed contract; its English and Simplified Chinese forms must remain behaviorally equivalent.
- Monitoring never journals every Market Tick or Level 2 update; those records stay in their owning Market evidence store and are referenced by identity and time.
- A pre-V1 schema change may invalidate old local data without migration or dual-read guarantees; explicit reset or replacement is acceptable.
- Supabase is not a research warehouse, and future QuestDB/ClickHouse-style services are deferred rather than pre-provisioned.
