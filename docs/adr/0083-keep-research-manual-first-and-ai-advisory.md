# Keep V1 research manual-first and AI advisory

ADAQ V1 uses one Host-owned, User-directed research workflow: frozen inputs produce immutable Dataset and Evaluation evidence, explicit Selection and Promotion Decisions unlock later Model, Strategy, Qualification, and Deployment gates, and Paper feedback starts new Attempts rather than mutating an active Bundle. A future AI Research Controller may propose work and request bounded mechanical Attempts through a scoped, expiring Research Automation Grant, but it is not an authority boundary: it cannot access unauthorized final-evaluation data, write stores or secrets, create User Decisions, alter policy or risk, delete evidence, or promote, qualify, deploy, or start a Bot automatically.

## Consequences

- AI output is an Advisory Artifact with recorded Surface, model, input, tool-call, hash, and User-decision provenance; it is not research evidence by itself.
- Trust, Parameter Selection, Promotion, Research Review, Qualification, Deployment, and Bot Start remain explicit User decisions.
- External AI data export, cross-User memory, parallel research stores or queues, and autonomous deployment require separate future capability and security decisions.
