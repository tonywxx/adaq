# Prioritize post-V1 personal use and Demo feedback

Status: accepted

The next development phase serves the Release Owner's sustained personal use of the delivered V1 workflow. Its goal is an independently completed OKX Demo research-to-execution loop with actual Demo Fills, traceable Paper feedback, and a human Research Review Decision that informs the next research iteration. Workflow effectiveness, rather than profitability, is the acceptance objective; detailed scenarios and evidence thresholds remain to be agreed.

Prioritize problems observed in this workflow before broadening markets or preparing external-user onboarding. This trades slower feature expansion for direct evidence of research usefulness and operational reliability. It does not authorize Live Trading or change the immutable feedback and human-owned promotion boundaries in ADR 0054. V1.1 is a working phase label, not a decision to publish a stable version or change compatibility policy.

The bounded EMA Double-Cross Paper Experiment is an explicit extension of ADR 0049's deferred decision-source boundary: its qualified event-driven Trade input may drive the forming-bar Strategy only inside this supervised Paper workflow. The extension preserves ADR 0049's causality, availability, Risk, OMS, reconciliation, and supervision rules; it does not enable general tick-driven Strategies or alter existing Closed-Bar and scheduled-batch Bots.
